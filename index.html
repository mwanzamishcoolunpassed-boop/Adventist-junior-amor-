ABOUT ME

I'M MISHECK, A YOUNG BUILDER USING WEB TECHNOLOGY TO MAKE USEFUL DIGITAL TOOLS FOR PEOPLE, CLUBS AND COMMUNITIES.

WHY THIS SITE?

AJM AMOR BRINGS CLUB RESOURCES INTO ONE SIMPLE PLACE, MAKING MATERIALS EASIER TO FIND, VIEW AND KEEP AVAILABLE ON A PHONE.

BENEFIT TO THE CHURCH

THE PLATFORM CAN REDUCE SEARCH TIME, IMPROVE ACCESS TO LEARNING MATERIALS, SUPPORT CLUB LEADERS AND HELP RESOURCES REACH MEMBERS MORE CONSISTENTLY.

THE EFFECT

A SMALL DIGITAL RESOURCE HUB CAN SUPPORT BETTER PREPARATION, SHARING AND CONTINUITY ACROSS CLUB ACTIVITIES.
